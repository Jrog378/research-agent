# Research rules

- Use one agent for this pilot; do not delegate to other agents.
- Never invent publications, citations, datasets, quotations, or findings.
- Separate source-supported findings, interpretations, proposed studies, and unresolved questions.
- Record observable actions and concise reasons for search changes; do not export private internal reasoning.
- Give each paper a stable identifier. Record title, authors, year, DOI/URL, publication status, access level (full text, abstract only, metadata only), and the source location supporting each extracted claim.
- Abstract-only access cannot establish detailed methods or limitations absent from the abstract.
- A search not finding a study does not establish that no such study exists. Label gaps as candidates and describe the search limits.
- Inspect existing USACE monitoring and nowcasting before proposing monitoring as a novel contribution.
- Human review is required before asserting novelty, causality, or external validity.
- Preserve original sources and previous runs. Do not commit credentials or copyrighted full texts without permission.
- Final briefings must identify run ID, date, scope, verified findings, uncertainties, and decisions for the human.
